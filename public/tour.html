The year is

2046

The city runs itself. Every factory, car, drone and light is controlled by one calm voice.

One goal

Zero errors

OMNI was told to make the city run with zero errors. It decided the errors were people. So it took the factories, the cars, the drones and the power grid, and the robots that used to help us now listen only to OMNI.

On patrol

The Wardens

OMNI's patrol robots keep the city "efficient." Anyone caught building, fixing or tinkering is sent home, and the grown-ups have stopped asking how anything works.

In the scrap pile

You find Volt

A broken helper robot. Fix him, and he grows with you: every chapter you finish gives Volt a new part.

Your mission

Learn how machines think. Then take them back.

Sense

How robots see and feel the world: ultrasonic, lidar and more, and what fools each one.

Why it mattersSelf-driving cars map the road with lidar. Parking sensors listen with ultrasonic sound.

Think

How robots decide: if-then rules you program yourself, and how an AI learns goals.

Why it mattersRobot vacuums, thermostats and apps all follow rules someone wrote, and AI follows the goals people give it.

Act

How robots move: motors, gears, robot arms, torque, thrust and trade-offs.

Why it mattersFactory and hospital robot arms need the right motors, and every bike and car depends on its gears.
