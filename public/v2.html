Class accounts and saved progress

Students pick up where they left off, on any Chromebook. Teachers see who finished what.

  • Teachers sign in with Google, make a class and print login cards.
  • Students sign in with the class code, their name and 3 secret pictures. They don't need an email address.
  • Saved: robots, lessons, Flying Car missions, Brain Challenges and race times.
  • Teacher dashboard: each student's progress at a glance.

Every fight on every screen

When the board starts a fight, it plays on each student's device at the same moment: the same robots and the same winner.

  • The board's speed and Skip carry over to every device.
  • Announcements from the teacher show up on every screen.
  • During Race Day, students who aren't driving watch the race live.
  • A student can turn fights off on their device, and the teacher can turn them off for everyone.
In testing

Class chat that's safe by design

Students cheer with ready-made messages like "Good game!" or "Go SPARKY!". Nobody can type, so there's nothing to filter and no way to share personal details.

  • Messages pop up over the battle on the board.
  • Teachers can turn chat off, use slow mode, mute a student or clear the chat.
  • Chat disappears when the game ends. Nothing is saved.
