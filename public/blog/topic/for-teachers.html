What is Circuit Rebels?

A free, browser-based robotics program for grades 5 to 8: 5-minute lessons, class games and a story, run from the teacher's board with a join code.

By Duron Epps | November 22, 2026

Free for every class

Try it with your students

Circuit Rebels is a free, browser-based robotics program for grades 5 to 8, made in New York City by Duron Epps. Students learn how machines sense, think and act through 5-minute lessons and class games. No student accounts are needed to play, and it runs on Chromebooks.
